Garrula is a modular language and interaction system designed to support structured communication, interpretation, and reasoning over human-generated text. Reflections on Explainable AI

A decade in explainable AI

Call it a kill switch, or call it AI governance.

Understanding AI models is central to humans staying in control of them. I've spent ten years researching how expert knowledge and AI development can move forward together. Here is what I've learned — in the order this field grew up.

In the news

Sept 2026 — Congress's AI Kill Switch Act (H.R. 9917) would force frontier labs to keep the power to shut a model down, while regulators turn explainability into a compliance requirement. Control starts with understanding.

Phase I · The reckoning
  1. 01

    Refusing the black box

    Saying no to opaque AI decisions was the first stepping stone. Requiring an explanation for every decision was recognised as essential to building the appropriate amount of trust.

  2. 02

    Trust can be manufactured

    Trust can be manufactured incorrectly. There is no single good explanation — the idea of an explanation is deeply subjective. An explanation engineered to look sensible can nudge a user into trusting a wrong decision.

Phase II · The blueprint
  1. 03

    Explanation by design

    Explanations should never be an afterthought. They must be consumed by both the AI and the human — acting as information bottlenecks inside the model — and can be made trustworthy by design.

  2. 04

    Grounded in expert knowledge

    We have worked hard, as humans, to understand the rules of the world we live in. Explanations must speak that same language — so the expert can make the final call.

  3. 05

    A double-edged sword

    Explanations do good when they keep AI safe — but the same map that reveals why a model decides also exposes the regions its predictions are most sensitive to, opening the door to attack.
